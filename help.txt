1. The Grays (Neutrals)
* slate (Coolest, slightly blueish - You are using this in your Origin Insights theme!)
* gray (Cool)
* zinc (Neutral/Default)
* neutral (True neutral)
* stone (Warmest, slightly brownish)

2. The Warm Colors
* orange
* amber (Slightly more golden/brown than yellow)
* rose (A deeper, slightly redder pink)
* pink
* fuchsia (Bright purplish-pink)

3. The Cool Colors
* purple
* violet (Slightly bluer than purple)
* sky (Lighter, brighter blue)
* cyan (Blue-green)
* teal (Darker blue-green)
* emerald (A cooler, slightly bluer green)
* lime (A bright, yellow-green)

4. The Base Colors
These do not use the numbered shade scale:
* black
* white
* transparent (Great for borders or invisible backgrounds)
* current (Inherits the current text color—useful for SVG icons)
